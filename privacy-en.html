Privacy Policy

Trash Day Cat / Last updated October 26, 2026

Trash Day Cat does not collect any information about you.

Your schedules and records are stored only on your device and are never sent to anyone, including the developer. There is no account to create.

What is stored on your device

To run the app and its widgets, the following is stored on your device: your cat's name and coat, trash types and pickup days, chores, reminder times, completion records, star count, room decorations, and shopping list. Deleting the app removes all of this.

Notifications

Reminders for trash days and chores are local notifications scheduled on your device. No information is sent to a server for notifications.

In-app purchases

Coats and furniture sets are purchased through Apple's App Store. The developer never receives your payment information. Purchase status is also checked between your device and the App Store.

Analytics and ads

The app contains no analytics tools and no ads.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date above.

Contact

Contact details are on the support page.
